## About Me

I am a second-year Master's student in Biomedical Engineering at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/) (UESTC), advised by Prof. Rong Li and Prof. Huafu Chen. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Communications Engineering from Chengdu University of Information Technology (CUIT) in 2024, ranking top 1% in my cohort.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **graph neural networks**, **brain imaging analysis**, and **LLM-based autonomous agents**. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that bridge structured biomedical data (brain networks, knowledge graphs) with modern foundation model capabilities.

---

## Research

My current research develops along two complementary lines:

**Brain Network Modeling with Graph Neural Networks.** I design GNN architectures for fMRI-based brain disorder diagnosis — including multi-hop spatio-temporal GCNs and multi-scale static-dynamic fusion networks — with a focus on cross-site generalization and interpretability. My work has been accepted at *Pattern Recognition* and is under review at *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*.

**LLM Agents for Autonomous Decision-Making.** I build agent systems that combine reinforcement learning with retrieval-augmented generation for multi-step reasoning and clinical applications. This includes a Mobile Agent with step-level PPO and an Agentic RAG system for epilepsy consultation using LangGraph orchestration.
