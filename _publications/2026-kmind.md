## Abstract

Existing multimodal fusion methods coarsely concatenate fMRI and sMRI features without incorporating validated medical knowledge to guide cross-modality interaction. K-MIND encodes PubMedBERT-derived semantic priors for 246 brain subregions, and fuses fMRI and sMRI connectivity via structure-guided projection, orthonormal clustered attention, and QFormer cross-modal interaction — knowledge explicitly directs modality attention at brain-region granularity. Evaluated on 2,350 subjects across three private TLE datasets and two public benchmarks (ABIDE, ADHD-200).
