## Abstract

Existing brain network models analyze static and dynamic functional connectivity at a single scale, failing to capture hierarchical spatiotemporal dependencies. We propose MSD-Net, integrating group-guided contrastive pooling (static) and diffusion spatial attention (dynamic) via cross-view fusion, with anatomical-functional dual-scale tokenization and local-global fusion. The model is optimized by RevCon and entropy-based sparsity regularization, achieving state-of-the-art performance on three private TLE datasets and two public benchmarks (ABIDE-I, ADHD-200). This work was presented at ISICDM 2025 and the 2026 Chinese Intelligent Health & Bioinformatics Conference.
